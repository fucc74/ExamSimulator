# Code of Conduct

We want QZ to be a friendly place for everyone, whatever their experience, background or identity.

**Expected behaviour**
- Be respectful and patient; assume good intent.
- Give and accept feedback graciously; focus on the work, not the person.
- Help newcomers.

**Unacceptable behaviour**
- Harassment, insults, discrimination, personal attacks, or publishing someone's private information.
- Sexualised language or imagery, trolling, or sustained disruption of discussions.

**Enforcement**
Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and contributions that break this code, and may restrict participation. To report a problem, contact the maintainer through the email on their GitHub profile.

This text is adapted from the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
